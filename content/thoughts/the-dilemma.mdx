---
title: The Dilemma
slug: the-dilemma
date: "2020-10"
description: A short poem on growing up, learning, and the messy honesty of feeling everything at once.
---

The world out there is nasty,<br />
I am still learning.

Why do people promise to take away the pain?<br />
When I know there's no gain, without pain.

When I am angry, I am loud and violent,<br />
When I am hurt, I am clear & silent.<br />
But people suffer from my pain,<br />
And this not what I wanna gain.

I wonder if this the Zeroth law?<br />
Are people gonna hate me for every flaw?<br />
As tear sweeps down my cheek,<br />
I am still learning not to be weak.

I wish there was a guide.<br />
But then, I won't be living in the pride.<br />
Is there an end to this learning cycle?<br />
Or am I gonna die letting my emotions recycle?
